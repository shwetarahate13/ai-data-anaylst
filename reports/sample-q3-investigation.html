Why did revenue drop in Q3?

confidence: high

Revenue fell 10.3% in Q3 2024 vs Q2 2024 ($1.11M to $997.1K, -$114.7K).

  • Where it came from: repeat customers in the West region moved -$149.8K, more than the entire net change (-$114.7K), because the rest of the business moved +$35.0K.
  • Volume vs value: lower order volume accounts for -$129.5K; basket value (AOV $137 to $139) accounts for +$14.8K.
  • Likely mechanism: average delivery time for that segment went from 4.2 days to 7.5 days (+3.3 days), versus +0.1 days for the rest of the business (z = 61). Average review rating in that segment also moved from 4.3 to 3.3 stars. This is a strong association, not proof of causation.
  • Context: versus the same period last year, Q3 2024 revenue was -1.3% (the preceding period was +16.9%), so this is not just seasonality.
  • What-if (estimate): cutting delivery time by 20% in West could have added about $58.7K (range $53.3K to $64.2K) of repeat-customer revenue.
